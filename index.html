The studio

Not the kind of gym where you put your headphones in and disappear.

Biomefit is a strength and fitness studio in the heart of Downtown Tampa's Water Street. Our classes are challenging without being intimidating — a mix of strength training, conditioning, and programming designed to keep things interesting. Come for Booty Babez, get stronger with Flex Training, work one-on-one with a coach, or mix it all together.

Workout

The workout

From dynamic classes to open gym, every square foot of the training floor is built to enhance physical results — the equipment and layout are the ideal setting for it.

Recovery

The recovery

Training effectively requires proper recovery. Contrast therapy on site — sauna and cold plunge — to hit optimal results and stay ahead of injury.

People

The people

Coaches who know your name and a room that fills up with regulars. Personalized programming when you want it, a crowd to push you when you don't.
