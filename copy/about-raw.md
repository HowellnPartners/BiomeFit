## Draft A — corporate

ready to take on the world. Discover the Biomefit difference and start your
fitness journey with us today.

## Draft C — the good one

> Your neighborhood training spot in Water Street.

Biomefit is a strength and fitness studio in the heart of Downtown Tampa's Water
Street — built for good workouts, good energy, and people who actually like
showing up.

Our classes are challenging without being intimidating, with a mix of strength
training, conditioning, and programming designed to keep things interesting. Come
for Booty Babez, get stronger with Flex Training, work one-on-one with a coach,

Because we're not interested in being the kind of gym where you put your
headphones in and disappear. Biomefit is somewhere you know your coaches,
recognize the people next to you, and leave feeling better than when you walked
in.

> Your new favorite place to sweat.

## Notes

**Use Draft C.** A and B are the generic wellness-brand voice — "thriving
ecosystem," "holistic well-being," "start your fitness journey" — interchangeable
with any studio in the country. C has an actual point of view and one real idea
worth building the site around: *this is not the gym where you disappear into
your headphones.* That is the differentiator, and it should drive the photography
selects too — faces, coaches, people talking, not solo-in-mirror shots.

**Salvage from A/B:** the two service pillars (group classes / personalized
training) are useful structure for a Home page section. The class names —
**Booty Babez**, **Flex Training** — are concrete and should appear early; they
match what's live on the Mariana Tek schedule.

**Spelling to settle:** copy uses both "Waterstreet" and "Water Street." The
district is officially **Water Street Tampa** (two words). Standardizing on that
unless the client objects.
