## Video specifics

A background hero video should be:

- **Muted and silent.** Browsers block autoplay with sound, so a video that
  depends on audio won't play at all.
- **Short.** 8–15 seconds, looping. Longer just means a bigger download.
- **Loopable.** It reads best if the end can cut back to the start without a
  visible jump. If it can't, I'll crossfade it.
- **Landscape, and safe in the middle.** It gets cropped hard on phones — a
  vertical slice out of the center is all that survives. Keep the subject
  centered, or send a separate vertical cut for mobile.
- **Legible under text.** The headline sits on top of it. Busy or bright footage
  fights the type; I'll darken it, but calmer footage looks better.

I'll produce the mp4 + webm and the poster frame. You don't need to.
