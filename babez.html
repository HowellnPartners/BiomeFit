The room

Nobody is watching you learn.

The whole floor is set up for the work — racks, benches and machines, all of it available, none of it queued behind someone waiting for you to finish. Come in knowing exactly what you're doing or come in knowing nothing at all.

The people

You'll know their names by week two.

The same faces turn up to the same slots. That's the point — it's a room you come back to, not one you disappear into. Coaches scale every movement, so the person next to you being stronger changes nothing about your session.
